Perfect SCP heuristic:
from integration to a tuned implementation

An autonomous optimization loop that integrated the perfect saturated cost partitioning heuristic (Höft, Speck & Seipp, KR 2025) into Scorpion and then reworked its construction until it fits in time and memory. Every kept change was A/B-measured against a frozen reference and confirmed on the full optimal-STRIPS benchmark set; initial heuristic values stayed bit-identical throughout. Opened as scorpion#33.

Three optimization campaigns

The loop ran in three segments, each re-baselined to the previous segment's end (ratio = 1.0), so the curves are within-segment and not directly comparable across panels. Lower is better.
